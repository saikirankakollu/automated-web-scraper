## Key Findings

1. **Only 10% of analyzed brands** in this batch have dedicated country-specific domains for the MENA region.
2. **UAE (.ae) leads again** with all 10 regional brands having UAE domains, consistent with the UAE's position as primary MENA e-commerce hub.
3. **Saudi Arabia (.sa)** has 7 brands with dedicated domains — large global lifestyle/tech brands (FILA, Flexispot, Fujifilm, ghd, Edifier, Forever52).
4. **Egypt (.eg)** has zero dedicated domains in this batch.
5. **27% of brands are marketplace-only** (Amazon/eBay sellers with no independent website), making regional domain analysis not applicable.
6. **15% are country-specific brands** from India, Turkey, Mexico, Japan, etc. — their existing ccTLD is for a non-MENA country.
7. **13% use global sites** with regional distribution through Sephora ME, Ounass, Amazon.ae, or other local retailers rather than dedicated regional domains.
8. **Key brands with both UAE + KSA**: Edifier, FILA, FLEXISPOT, Forever52, FUJI/Fujifilm, ghd — all confirmed active with separate localized stores.

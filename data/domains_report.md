## Key Findings

1. **Only 12.1% of analyzed brands** have dedicated country-specific domains for the MENA region.
2. **UAE (.ae) leads** with 23 brands having dedicated domains, reflecting the UAE's status as the primary e-commerce hub in the region.
3. **Saudi Arabia (.sa)** has 15 brands with dedicated domains, typically the larger global brands.
4. **Egypt (.eg)** has minimal presence with only 2 brands (Bostik and Deborah Milano) having dedicated Egyptian domains.
5. **Most brands** rely on global websites, regional path selectors (e.g., `/en-ae`), or third-party retailers (Amazon.ae, Noon, Sephora ME) rather than dedicated country domains.
6. **Luxury/fashion brands** (Burberry, Dolce & Gabbana, Calvin Klein, Clinique) and **tech/lifestyle brands** (FILA, Flexispot, Edifier, Fujifilm, ghd) are most likely to invest in regional domains.
7. **UAE-native brands** (Cool & Cool, Emirates Macaroni, Cosmoplast, Emirates airline) naturally have .ae domains.
